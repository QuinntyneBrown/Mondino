# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Mondino is where a team spread around the world sees each other. A teammate joins with a name
and a postal code and appears as a pin on a 3D globe, with their local time, their weather and
whatever is happening where they live. It exists to give teammates something to talk about and
a way to get to know each other.

These requirements use the words defined in `AGENTS.md` (teammate, team, invite link, pin,
postal code, city, globe, local time, weather, status, offset, overlap, moment, kind). The
detailed requirements are in [L2.md](./L2.md).

**Not specified yet.** Three things are not designed, so no requirement here covers them: how
a teammate who joined by invite link, and so gave no email, signs in again; what a sign-in link
shows once it has expired or been used; and what "Reset link" asks before it acts
(`docs/mocks/README.md` lists them). Mondino has no chat, no calendar or meeting scheduler, no
presence or activity tracking, no precise location, no social feed, no adverts, and no admins or
roles; the prototype's "Message" button is not built.

## L1-001: Join a team with a name and a postal code
A person who opens a team's invite link must be able to put themselves on that team's globe by
giving only a name, a country and a postal code. Mondino must work for teammates in any
supported country, starting with at least Canada, the United States and Italy.

## L1-002: Only the city is shown
A teammate's location must be detailed enough to tell neighbouring cities apart (Mississauga
from Toronto) and no more detailed than that. A postal code places a pin at its city and stays
private: no teammate ever sees another's postal code, address or exact position.

## L1-003: A beautiful 3D globe
The team must be shown on a three-dimensional globe with the soft, stylised, toy-like look of a
modern animated film, built from assets modelled in Blender. The globe turns slowly on its own,
is dragged to spin, turns to a selected pin, and shades its night side.

## L1-004: A pin for every teammate
Every teammate must appear on the globe as a pin that identifies them and shows their local
time and weather. Pins must stay distinguishable on a small globe: a pin that is pointed at,
focused or selected lifts out from the globe and shows its teammate clearly.

## L1-005: Local time, status, offset and overlap
Mondino must show each teammate's local time, the status that follows from their local hour,
how far ahead or behind they are compared with the teammate looking, and the working hours the
two share.

## L1-006: Weather at each pin
Mondino must show the current temperature and weather conditions at each teammate's city, in
the unit the viewing teammate chooses.

## L1-007: Moments where teammates live
Mondino must show what is happening where a teammate lives (a holiday, Formula 1, a local
event or sport) as a moment, and must mark that teammate's pin on the globe with a
marker that identifies the moment.

## L1-008: A teammate's details and the week around the world
From the globe, a teammate must be able to open any teammate and see their city, local time,
offset, status, weather, moments and overlap, and, with no pin selected, see the moments of the
coming week and who is in working hours now.

## L1-009: The team through its day
Mondino must list every teammate from west to east, placed at the hour of the day they are
living through, and let the list be narrowed to those working now or asleep.

## L1-010: A list of moments
Mondino must list the team's upcoming moments by when they happen (today, this week, later) and
let the list be narrowed by kind.

## L1-011: A teammate's own pin and settings
A teammate must be able to see and change their own location, choose how temperatures and times
are displayed, choose which kinds of moment show on the globe, choose what they share, and
remove their pin and leave the team.

## L1-012: Responsive from phone to large screen
Every screen must work at every viewport width: extra small (phone, < 576 px), small
(≥ 576 px), medium (tablet, ≥ 768 px), large (desktop, ≥ 992 px) and extra large (≥ 1200 px).

## L1-013: Accessible to everyone
Mondino must meet WCAG 2.2 level AA: every interaction works by keyboard and by touch,
everything shown on the globe is also available as text, and motion is reduced for those who
ask for it.

## L1-014: Secure by default
A teammate must see only the team they are in and change only their own pin. Everything a
person types is validated and treated as data. Anything open to anyone is rate limited.

## L1-015: Fast, and light for a whole working day
Mondino must load quickly on a phone, keep the globe smooth on an ordinary laptop, and use no
more memory at the end of a day left open in a tab than at the start.

## L1-016: Dependable when an outside source fails
Mondino depends on outside sources for postal codes, weather and calendars. When one is slow or
fails, the team must still see every pin and every local time.

## L1-017: Observable without exposing teammates
Mondino must be observable in production, and nothing it records may identify a teammate or
where they are.

## L1-018: Make a team and bring everyone in
A person must be able to make a team by giving a team name, their own name, a work email, a
country and a postal code. The team is made with its maker's pin on it and an invite link, and
any teammate can open that invite link from any screen to copy it and send it on. Whoever makes
a team is a teammate like any other.

## L1-019: Come back with an emailed link
A teammate who gave a work email must be able to come back on another browser or device with no
password: Mondino emails a link that signs them in once, within 15 minutes. Mondino never tells
anyone whether it knows an email.
