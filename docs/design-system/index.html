Components

Actions

ButtonA button does something: places a pin, joins a team, saves a change, tries again.LinkA link goes somewhere: to Moments, to Profile & Settings, back to the globe.

Navigation

Segmented controlOne pill holding a few options, exactly one of them chosen: the header’s tabs, the Team filter, a two-way setting.Skip linkThe first thing Tab reaches on every screen: a link straight to the content.App headerThe bar across the top of every screen after joining: the mark, Globe, Team and Moments, and the way to your own pin.

Inputs

Text fieldOne line of text a teammate types: their name, their postal code.SelectOne choice from a list too long to show: the country.Form fieldThe wrapper every input sits in: its label above, its message below.Lookup messageThe line under the postal code that says what Mondino did with it: kept it private, is finding it, found the city, or could not.SwitchOne setting that is on or off: a kind of moment on the globe, something a teammate shares.ChipA filter that is pressed or not: the kinds of moment.

Data display

AvatarA teammate’s initials in their own colour, wherever they appear off the globe.StatusWhere a teammate is in their day, in words: Working hours, Early morning, Evening, Probably asleep.EmblemA moment’s icon on a disc of sun: what kind of moment it is, at a glance.

Containers

CardA surface that groups one thing: the invite link, or your own pin.

Overlays

DialogA question that must be answered before anything else: leaving the team, or leaving unsaved changes.

Feedback

ToastA short confirmation that rises from the bottom of the screen: the invite link was copied, or could not be.AlertA message in place about something that needs attention: a join that failed, a full team, a source that did not answer.Save barThe bar on Profile & Settings that says a change is not saved yet, is being saved, is saved, or could not be.SkeletonThe shape of what is coming, shown while a screen’s first data loads.SpinnerA turning ring that says one action is in flight.Empty stateWhat a screen says when it has nothing to show, could not load, or is not for this person.

Brand

LogoThe mark, a little world with one pin on its rim, and the lowercase wordmark mondino.

Mondino

GlobeThe little world itself: every teammate’s pin on it, its night side shaded.PinA teammate on the globe: their initials in their own colour.Pin labelThe chip beside a pin: the teammate’s local time, weather and temperature.Moment markerThe small sun disc beside a pin that says something is happening there this week.Panel rowA line in the panel that selects a teammate: a moment this week, or someone in working hours now.Teammate panelA selected teammate, answered in one column: where they are, what time it is there, and what it is like.Moment cardA moment on soft sun, in a teammate’s panel; and its one-line form, the pill, in the Team table.Moment rowA moment in the Moments list: its date, what it is, and whose it is. Today’s moment is the same thing made large.Overlap barThe part of your working day and a teammate’s that coincides, drawn on your own clock.Day ribbonThe 24 hours of a day, with each teammate placed at the hour they are living through right now.Team tableEveryone, west to east: each teammate’s location, local time, offset from you, weather and next moment.

Patterns

FormsMondino has two forms: joining a team, and your own settings. Both ask for very little and lose nothing.Feedback and loadingEvery action answers at once, where the teammate is looking, and waiting always says what it is waiting for.Empty and error statesWhat Mondino says when there is nothing to show, when it could not load, and when a screen is not for this person.Navigation and page structureFive screens and one header. The whole app stays this small.Dialogs and overlaysMondino interrupts twice only. Everything else happens in place.Data tables and listsHow Mondino lists teammates and moments: in an order that means something, with a row that opens the globe.NotificationsMondino sends nothing: no e-mail, no push, no badge. It only tells you what just happened on the screen you are looking at.Content and toneA small, warm product about people far apart. It speaks plainly and never claims to know more than a clock and a calendar can tell.

How to use

  1. Link the tokens, then the components:
    <link rel="stylesheet" href="tokens/tokens.css">
<link rel="stylesheet" href="assets/components.css">
  2. Use a component’s classes and attributes as its page gives them. The mocks’ BEM classes are kept: the end-to-end tests locate by them.
  3. Read tokens by role (var(--color-fg-muted)); never write a colour, a size or a time.
  4. For another platform, generate constants from tokens.json.
  5. In the Angular app each component here is one presentation component in components, with one encapsulated BEM stylesheet; global styles hold the tokens, the fonts and the reset only.
