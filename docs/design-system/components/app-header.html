App header

The bar across the top of every screen after joining: the mark, Globe, Team and Moments, and the way to your own pin.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

The header is the whole of Mondino’s navigation. Its tabs are a segmented control of links, and the avatar at the right opens Profile & Settings. Join and the pages for someone in no team use the bare header: the mark and wordmark alone.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. Mark and wordmarkHome; the wordmark shows from 576 px
  2. TabsGlobe, Team, Moments
  3. Invite teammateFrom 768 px
  4. Your avatarProfile & Settings; ringed in pin when current

Sizes

It has one size.

States

The header itself has no states. Its tabs, button and avatar link each have their own, on their pages.

Responsive behaviour

Under 576 px: the mark without the wordmark, the tabs, and the avatar. From 576 px the lowercase wordmark joins the mark. From 768 px “Invite teammate” appears. Under 360 px the gaps and the tabs’ padding tighten so it fits 320 px. It is 64 px tall at every width.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <header> holding a <nav aria-label="Primary">. The current screen’s link has aria-current="page", including the avatar link on Profile & Settings.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabHome, Globe, Team, Moments, Invite teammate, Profile & Settings, in that order
EnterFollows the focused link

Focus

The shared ring on each link. The skip link comes before the header.

Labelling

The mark’s link is named “Mondino home”. The avatar’s link is named “Profile & Settings”, not the teammate’s initials.

Contrast

Current tab --color-fg-on-accent on --color-accent
Other tabs --color-fg-muted on --color-bg-surface
Current avatar ring --color-people on --color-bg-canvas

Motion

Colour and size changes take --duration-fast. Reduced motion sets every duration token to nothing, so the change is instant with no extra rule.

Touch

The target is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-comfortable).

Content

The tabs are “Globe”, “Team” and “Moments”. The wordmark is always lowercase mondino.
