Save bar

The bar on Profile & Settings that says a change is not saved yet, is being saved, is saved, or could not be.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

A change in Profile & Settings is not applied until it is saved. The bar appears the moment a setting differs from what was saved, and goes if it is changed back. It floats at the bottom of the screen so it is in reach wherever the teammate has scrolled.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. MessageThe state, in words
  2. Action“Save changes” or “Try again”

Variants

You have unsaved changes
Saving…
Saved — your pin is up to date
Couldn’t save. Your changes are still here.
Unsaved, saving, saved, could not save

Sizes

It has one size.

States

Its four variants are its states; each is shown under Variants.

Responsive behaviour

Fixed at the bottom centre: 16 px from each side on a phone, at most --layout-overlay-max wide. The message wraps to two lines before the button shrinks.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="status", so each change of message is announced politely; role="alert" when the save failed.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the bar’s button after the last setting
Enter / SpaceSaves, or tries again

Focus

Appearing never moves focus. After a save, focus stays on the setting that was last changed.

Labelling

The message is the announcement. While saving, the button carries aria-busy="true".

Contrast

Message --color-fg-inverse on --color-bg-inverse
Button --color-fg-default on --color-bg-canvas
Icon --color-fg-inverse-accent on --color-bg-inverse

Motion

Rises 12 px and fades in over --duration-base. “Saved” dismisses itself after 4 seconds; a failure stays.

Touch

The target is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-comfortable).

Content

The four messages are fixed: “You have unsaved changes” with “Save changes”; “Saving…”, with the button reading “Saving” beside a spinner; “Saved — your pin is up to date”; “Couldn’t save. Your changes are still here.” with “Try again”.
