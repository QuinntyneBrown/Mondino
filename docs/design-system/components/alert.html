Alert

A message in place about something that needs attention: a join that failed, a full team, a source that did not answer.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

An alert sits where the problem is and stays until it is resolved. It says what happened and what to do, and carries the action when there is one. A banner is an alert across the top of a screen for something that affects the whole screen. When the whole form cannot be used (the invite link no longer works, the team is full) a notice takes the form’s place instead of an alert above it.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. IconOne per tone
  2. MessageWhat happened, in bold when more follows, and what it means
  3. Action (optional)A small button

Sizes

It has one size.

States

An alert is shown or gone. Its button has the states of a button.

Responsive behaviour

An alert is the full width of its column and its text wraps. A banner’s button drops under its text when they do not fit one line.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="alert" when it appears in answer to something the teammate did and needs them now (a failed join, a postal code not found). role="status" for a banner. No role when it is part of the page as it loads.

Keyboard

The alert takes no keys; its button and links do.

Focus

A form’s error summary takes focus when the form is submitted with errors. No other alert moves focus.

Labelling

Tone is carried by the icon and the words, never by colour alone.

Contrast

Danger --color-danger-fg on --color-danger-bg
Warning --color-fg-default on --color-warning-bg
Information --color-info-fg on --color-info-bg

Motion

None.

Touch

The target is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-comfortable).

Content

Say what happened, then what it means for the teammate, then what to do: “We couldn’t add you to Northwind. Your details are still here — try joining again.” When a source fails, say it could not be checked, not that the teammate was wrong: “We couldn’t check M5V 2T6 right now. Your postal code may be fine — nothing is wrong with what you typed. Your pin stays in Milan.” A wait gives its length and counts down: “Too many tries from this device. You can try again in 9:42.” Never show a code, a stack trace or the backend’s own text.

The banner says what is still true and how old the rest is: “Couldn’t refresh. Clocks are live; weather and moments are from 14:50.” Its button reads “Try again”, and “Trying…” meanwhile.
