Toast

A short confirmation that rises from the bottom of the screen: the invite link was copied or could not be, or you have just joined.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

A toast confirms something the teammate just did when nothing on the screen changes to show it. Mondino has three: the invite link was copied, it could not be copied, and “You’re on the map.” after joining. A saved setting is confirmed by the save bar, and a problem with the page by an alert.

A success is the inverse surface, ink in light. A failure is not ink: it is a raised surface with a pin edge and a danger icon, so it cannot be mistaken for a confirmation, and it stays until it is dismissed.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. IconA tick, or an alert in the danger colour
  2. TextOne paragraph: what happened, in bold when a second sentence follows, then what to do next
  3. DismissAlways present
  4. EdgeA failure only: a 2 px pin edge around a raised surface

Variants

Invite link copied. Paste it wherever your team talks.

You’re on the map. Northwind can now see your pin in Milan.

Couldn’t copy the link. It’s selected — press Ctrl+C or ⌘C to copy it yourself.

Sizes

It has one size.

States

A toast is shown or gone. A success leaves by itself after 4 seconds; a failure stays until it is dismissed. Its dismiss button has the states of an icon button.

Invite link copied. Paste it wherever your team talks.

Couldn’t copy the link. It’s selected — press Ctrl+C or ⌘C to copy it yourself.

Responsive behaviour

Bottom centre at every width, 16 px from each side on a phone and at most --layout-overlay-max wide.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

It appears in a region that is already in the page: role="status" with aria-live="polite" for a success; role="alert" with aria-live="assertive" for a failure.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches Dismiss
Enter / SpaceDismisses the toast

Focus

Focus stays where it was; a toast never takes it. When copying the invite link is refused, focus is in the invite dialog on the selected link, not on the toast.

Labelling

The text is announced once. Dismiss is named “Dismiss”. A failure differs from a success by its surface, its edge, its icon and its words, not by colour alone.

Contrast

Success: text --color-fg-inverse on --color-bg-inverse
Success: icon --color-fg-inverse-accent on --color-bg-inverse
Failure: text --color-fg-default on --color-bg-surface-raised
Failure: icon --color-danger-icon on --color-bg-surface-raised
Failure: edge against the page --color-danger-border on --color-bg-canvas

Motion

Rises in --duration-base. A success leaves by itself after 4 seconds, and the timer stops while it is pointed at or focused. A failure stays until dismissed. Under reduced motion it appears in place.

Touch

The target is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-comfortable).

Content

One or two short sentences in one paragraph. A success says what happened and, if it helps, what to do with it: “Invite link copied. Paste it wherever your team talks.”, “You’re on the map. Northwind can now see your pin in Milan.” A failure says what did not happen in bold, then what to do instead: “Couldn’t copy the link. It’s selected — press Ctrl+C or ⌘C to copy it yourself.”
