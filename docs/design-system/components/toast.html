Toast

A short confirmation that rises from the bottom of the screen: the invite link was copied, or could not be.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

A toast confirms something the teammate just did when nothing on the screen changes to show it. Mondino has one: copying the invite link. A saved setting is confirmed by the save bar, and a problem with the page by an alert.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. IconA tick, or an alert
  2. TitleWhat happened
  3. TextWhat to do next
  4. DismissAlways present

Variants

Invite link copied

Send it to a teammate. They add their pin with a name and a postal code.

Couldn’t copy the invite link

Your browser didn’t allow it. Select the link in the card and copy it yourself.

Sizes

It has one size.

States

A toast is shown or gone. Its dismiss button has the states of an icon button.

Responsive behaviour

Bottom centre at every width, 16 px from each side on a phone and at most --layout-overlay-max wide.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

It appears in a region that is already in the page: role="status", polite, for a success; role="alert" for a failure.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches Dismiss
Enter / SpaceDismisses the toast

Focus

Focus stays where it was; a toast never takes it.

Labelling

The title and text are announced once. Dismiss is named “Dismiss”.

Contrast

Text --color-fg-inverse on --color-bg-inverse
Icon --color-fg-inverse-accent on --color-bg-inverse

Motion

Rises in --duration-base. A success leaves by itself after 4 seconds, and the timer stops while it is pointed at or focused. A failure stays until dismissed.

Touch

The target is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-comfortable).

Content

Title: what happened, in a few words. Text: the next step. A failure says why and what to do instead.
