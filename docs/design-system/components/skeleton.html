Skeleton

The shape of what is coming, shown while a screen’s first data loads.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

A skeleton holds the place of content with the same layout, so nothing shifts when the content arrives. Every screen has a loading state built from skeletons; the globe’s is a plain disc in its place. For an action in flight on a button or a field, use a spinner.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. AvatarA disc, the avatar’s size
  2. Title line65% wide
  3. Second line40% wide
  4. FigureWhere a time or a date will be

Variants

Title, text, pill, control, and a composed row
HTML
<span class="skeleton skeleton--title"></span><span class="skeleton skeleton--text"></span><span class="skeleton skeleton--pill"></span><span class="skeleton skeleton--control"></span><div class="skeleton-row" style="width: 100%;"><span class="skeleton skeleton--avatar"></span><div class="skeleton-row__body"><span class="skeleton skeleton--mid"></span><span class="skeleton skeleton--short"></span></div><span class="skeleton skeleton--num"></span></div>

Sizes

It has one size.

States

A skeleton has one state. It pulses between full and half opacity.

Responsive behaviour

A skeleton follows the layout of the screen it stands in for at every width, including the panel moving under the globe.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Skeletons carry no meaning. Their container has aria-busy="true", and a visually hidden role="status" says what is loading (“Loading your team…”).

Keyboard

It is not focusable and takes no keys; it is read in place by a screen reader.

Focus

Not focusable. Real controls in the header stay usable while a screen loads.

Labelling

One status message per loading screen, not one per skeleton.

Contrast

Skeleton on the page --color-bg-subtle on --color-bg-canvas

Motion

A slow pulse of opacity over --duration-deliberate. Under reduced motion it stands still.

Touch

Not a control.

Content

The hidden status names the thing: “Loading your team…”, “Loading moments…”, “Opening your invite link…”.
