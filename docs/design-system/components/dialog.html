Dialog

A surface over the page that holds focus until it is answered or closed: inviting teammates, leaving the team, or leaving unsaved changes.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

A dialog interrupts. Mondino uses it three times only: to share the team’s invite link, to confirm leaving a team, and to ask before unsaved settings are lost. Everything else happens in place. See dialogs and overlays.

The invite dialog is something the teammate asked for, so it has a close button and nothing to confirm. The other two are questions that must be answered: they are alert dialogs, with two buttons and no close button.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. TitleWhat it is for, naming the team; a question in an alert dialog
  2. CloseOnly where there is nothing to answer: the invite dialog
  3. BodyExactly what happens, and what stays private or can be undone
  4. Label and contentIn the invite dialog: “Invite link” and a copy field
  5. MetaIn the invite dialog: who is on the team, and “Reset link”
  6. FooterIn an alert dialog: the safe action, then the confirming one

Responsive behaviour

Under 576 px the dialog is a sheet that rises from the bottom edge, the full width, with its buttons stacked and the confirming action on top. From 576 px it is centred, at most --layout-overlay-max wide, with its buttons in a row at the right. The invite dialog’s foot wraps: the count on one line and “Reset link” on the next when they cannot share one. The invite link in its copy field ends in an ellipsis when it is too long; it is never wrapped.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The invite dialog is WAI-ARIA Dialog (modal): role="dialog", aria-modal="true", named by its title and described by its first paragraph. Leaving the team and leaving unsaved changes are WAI-ARIA Alert Dialog: role="alertdialog", since each interrupts with a question that must be answered. The page behind is inert. Build both on CDK Dialog; never hand-roll a modal.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift + TabMoves between the dialog’s controls and stays inside it
EscCloses it; nothing has changed
EnterActivates the focused button
Ctrl + C / ⌘ + CIn the invite dialog, with the link selected after a refused copy: copies it

Focus

An alert dialog opens with focus on the safe action: “Stay”, “Keep editing”. The invite dialog opens with focus on “Copy link”. After a failed leave, focus is on “Try again”. On closing, focus returns to the button that opened it.

Labelling

The title names the dialog; the body is its description. The close button is named “Close”. The invite link has a visible label, “Invite link”. A failure is an alert at the top of the body.

Contrast

Body --color-fg-muted on --color-bg-surface-raised
Title --color-fg-default on --color-bg-surface-raised
Danger button --color-fg-on-danger on --color-danger-solid

Motion

It appears in --duration-slow: scaling from the centre, or rising as a sheet. No motion under reduced motion.

Touch

The target is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-comfortable).

Content

An alert dialog’s title is a question that names the thing: “Leave Northwind?”, “Leave without saving?”. The body says exactly what is lost and what is not: “Your pin comes off the globe for all 12 teammates, along with your weather and moments. To come back you’ll need the team’s invite link.” Buttons repeat the verb: “Stay” and “Leave Northwind”, “Discard and leave” and “Keep editing”. Never “Are you sure?”, “Yes”, “No” or “OK”.

The invite dialog’s title says what it does and for which team: “Invite teammates to Northwind”. Its body repeats the privacy promise: “They place their own pin with a postal code — only their city is ever shown.” Its foot reads “13 on the team · link never expires”. While leaving, the button reads “Leaving…”; if it fails, “We couldn’t take your pin off. You’re still on Northwind — try again.” and “Try again”.
