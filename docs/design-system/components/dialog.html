Dialog

A question that must be answered before anything else: leaving the team, or leaving unsaved changes.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

A dialog interrupts. Mondino uses it twice only: to confirm removing a pin and leaving a team, and to ask before unsaved settings are dropped. Everything else happens in place. See dialogs and overlays.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. TitleThe question, naming the team
  2. BodyExactly what happens, and what can be undone
  3. Safe actionCancel; takes focus first
  4. Confirming actionSays what it does; danger when it removes

Variants

Leave Northwind?

Your pin will be removed from the globe for everyone in Northwind, and Mondino will delete your name, postal code, city and settings for this team. You can join again with the team’s invite link.

Destructive: a danger button, focus on Cancel

Responsive behaviour

Under 576 px the dialog is a sheet that rises from the bottom edge, the full width, with its buttons stacked and the confirming action on top. From 576 px it is centred, at most --layout-overlay-max wide, with its buttons in a row at the right.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

WAI-ARIA Dialog (modal): role="dialog", aria-modal="true", named by its title and described by its body. The page behind is inert. Build it on CDK Dialog; never hand-roll a modal.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift + TabMoves between the dialog’s controls and stays inside it
EscCloses it; nothing has changed
EnterActivates the focused button

Focus

Opens with focus on the safe action. On closing, focus returns to the button that opened it.

Labelling

The title is the question; the body is its description. A failure is an alert at the top of the body.

Contrast

Body --color-fg-muted on --color-bg-surface-raised
Title --color-fg-default on --color-bg-surface-raised
Danger button --color-fg-on-danger on --color-danger-solid

Motion

It appears in --duration-slow: scaling from the centre, or rising as a sheet. No motion under reduced motion.

Touch

The target is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-comfortable).

Content

The title is a question that names the thing: “Leave Northwind?”. The body says exactly what is lost and what is not. Buttons repeat the verb: “Remove my pin and leave”, “Keep editing”. Never “Are you sure?”, “Yes” or “No”.
