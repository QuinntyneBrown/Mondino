Moment row

A moment in the Moments list: its date, what it is, and whose it is. Today’s moment is the same thing made large.

Overview

The Moments screen lists the team’s moments from today to 90 days ahead. A moment today is shown first and most prominently, on solid sun. The rest are rows grouped under “This week” and “Later”, in date order. Choosing one opens the Globe with its teammate’s pin selected.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. Date blockWeekday, day, month
  2. Kind and whenIcon, “Local event · In 4 days”
  3. TitleGabarito, 18 px
  4. DetailOne line
  5. WhoseAvatar, first name and city

Responsive behaviour

Under 576 px a row is two columns, the date block and the text, with whose it is on a line under the text; today’s moment stacks its emblem, text and “On the globe”. From 576 px whose it is moves to the right of the row and today’s moment lies in one line. The list keeps a reading measure, --layout-container-reading.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A link in a list, under a group heading (“This week”, “Later”). A group with nothing in it has no heading.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves moment to moment
EnterOpens the Globe with that teammate’s pin selected

Focus

The shared ring around the whole row.

Labelling

The whole row is the link’s name: kind, when, title, detail, teammate and city.

Contrast

Today’s title on sun --color-fg-on-moment on --color-moment
Today’s detail on sun --color-fg-moment-muted on --color-moment
Row detail --color-fg-muted on --color-bg-canvas

Motion

Colour and size changes take --duration-fast. Reduced motion sets every duration token to nothing, so the change is instant with no extra rule.

Touch

The target is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-comfortable).

Content

Today’s moment is headed “Today in” and its city. “Today” is the date in the moment’s own place. Rows read “Tomorrow”, “In 3 days”, beside the weekday, day and month.
