Teammate panel

A selected teammate, answered in one column: where they are, what time it is there, and what it is like.

Overview

Selecting a pin opens that teammate in the panel: name, city and country, local time and day, offset from you, status, weather, upcoming moments and the overlap of your working hours. Your own panel has “(you)” after the name and no offset or overlap.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. Avatar and nameGabarito, 20 px
  2. CloseClears the selection
  3. City and countryNever a postal code
  4. Local timeGabarito bold, 64 px, tabular
  5. Day and offsetTheir own day; “7 h behind you”
  6. StatusIn words
  7. WeatherIcon, temperature with unit, the sky in words

Responsive behaviour

Under 992 px the panel is under the globe in one scrolling column. From 992 px it is --layout-panel-width wide to the right of the globe and scrolls on its own while the globe stays in view.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An <article> named by the teammate’s name, inside an <aside>. Its change is announced once through a polite live region.

Keyboard

KeyAction
EscCloses the panel and clears the selection
TabClose, then Message

Focus

Opened from the keyboard, focus moves to the name. On closing it returns to the pin.

Labelling

The close button names the teammate: “Close Tomás Herrera”. The overlap is given as a sentence as well as a bar.

Contrast

Local time --color-fg-default on --color-bg-surface
Secondary text --color-fg-muted on --color-bg-surface

Motion

One teammate fades to the next over --duration-base, 180 ms. No fade under reduced motion. The clock ticking over is not announced.

Touch

The target is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-comfortable).

Content

Offset: “7 h behind you”, “4 h 30 min ahead of you”, “Same time as you”; never “0 hours” and never a sign. Weather: the temperature with its unit, then the sky and the city: “Partly cloudy in Mexico City”.
