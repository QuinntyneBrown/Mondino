Switch

One setting that is on or off: a kind of moment on the globe, something a teammate shares.

Overview

A switch is a whole row: the setting’s name, an optional hint, the word “On” or “Off”, and the track. It changes nothing until the teammate saves; the save bar appears as soon as it differs from what was saved.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. LabelThe setting, not the action
  2. Hint (optional)What it means for teammates
  3. State in words“On” or “Off”
  4. Track and thumbLeaf when on

Responsive behaviour

The row is the full width of its section at every width, so the whole line is the target and the track stays at the right edge.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <button role="switch"> with aria-checked (WAI-ARIA Switch).

Keyboard

KeyAction
Space / EnterTurns the setting on or off
TabMoves to the next switch

Focus

The shared ring around the whole row.

Labelling

The label is the accessible name and the hint is part of it. “On” and “Off” are drawn for sighted teammates and hidden from assistive technology, which reads the state from aria-checked.

Contrast

Track, on --color-working on --color-bg-canvas
Track, off --color-border-strong on --color-bg-canvas
Hint --color-fg-muted on --color-bg-canvas

Motion

Colour and size changes take --duration-fast. Reduced motion sets every duration token to nothing, so the change is instant with no extra rule.

Touch

The whole row is the target, at least 44 px tall.

Content

Name the setting as a thing that is true when on: “Share my local weather”, not “Hide my weather” or “Toggle weather”. The hint says what teammates see.
