Day ribbon

The 24 hours of a day, with each teammate placed at the hour they are living through right now; for a large team, how many teammates are at each half hour.

Overview

The ribbon heads the Team screen. It answers “where is everyone in their day?” at a glance: night, morning, working hours and evening are bands, and each teammate is an avatar at their local hour. Teammates at the same hour stack in rows so each can be chosen.

Past 40 teammates the avatars would no longer fit, and the ribbon becomes a bar chart over the same bands: 48 bars, one for each half hour of the day, each as tall as the share of the team living through that half hour. A line above it says how many are in working hours, and the tallest bar carries its count.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. Night band23:00 to 07:00, labelled
  2. Working-hours band09:00 to 18:00, on leaf, labelled
  3. MarkerA teammate at their local hour
  4. ScaleEvery three hours; every six on a phone

Responsive behaviour

With markers, the ribbon always fits the width; it never scrolls sideways. Under 768 px markers are 26 px and the “Morning” and “Evening” labels are dropped, keeping “Night” and “Working hours”. Under 576 px the scale shows every six hours. Markers within about two and a half hours of each other stack in rows, and the track grows to hold them.

As a bar chart the track is --ribbon-density-height tall plus room for the band labels. Forty-eight bars need room: the chart keeps a minimum width of 720 px inside its own scrolling group (.day-ribbon__scroll), so on a phone the chart scrolls sideways and the page does not. The summary line above it never scrolls.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A section with a hidden heading, holding a list of links. Each marker is a link named with the teammate and their local time.

As a bar chart the track is one role="img" named with what it shows and its peak: “How many teammates are at each half hour of their day. The most, 123, are at 15:00.” The bars are hidden from assistive technology, and the summary line gives the working-hours count as text. The scrolling group is focusable and named “The day ribbon; scroll sideways to see all 24 hours”.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves marker to marker, in order of local hour
EnterOpens the Globe with that teammate’s pin selected
← / →With focus on the bar chart’s scrolling group, scrolls it sideways

Focus

The shared ring around the marker.

Labelling

Bands are named in text, not by colour alone. A teammate who does not share whether they are working or asleep is still placed by their hour, and still counted in their half hour’s bar. Bars are one colour: their height carries the number, and the bands behind them say which part of the day it is.

Contrast

Band label on leaf --color-fg-muted on --color-working-subtle
Band label on soft sun --color-fg-muted on --color-moment-subtle
Bars on the working-hours band --color-fg-default on --color-working-subtle
Bars on the morning and evening bands --color-fg-default on --color-moment-subtle
Count over the tallest bar --color-fg-default on --color-working-subtle

Motion

A marker grows slightly when pointed at. Markers move as the clock ticks, without animation.

Touch

Markers are 26 px drawn on a phone; rows are 40 px apart so neighbours do not overlap.

Content

Band labels: “Night”, “Morning”, “Working hours”, “Evening”. The scale follows the viewer’s clock: “09:00” or “9 am”. The summary of a large team: “324 of 500 are in working hours right now”. Only the tallest bar carries its count.

.day-ribbon__trackThe 24-hour track; --rows sets its height.
.day-ribbon__band--night / --edge / --workingBands, placed by --from and --span.
.day-ribbon__markerA teammate; --at is the hour as a percentage, --row the row.
.day-ribbon__scaleThe hour marks.
