Day ribbon

The 24 hours of a day, with each teammate placed at the hour they are living through right now.

Overview

The ribbon heads the Team screen. It answers “where is everyone in their day?” at a glance: night, morning, working hours and evening are bands, and each teammate is an avatar at their local hour. Teammates at the same hour stack in rows so each can be chosen.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. Night band23:00 to 07:00, labelled
  2. Working-hours band09:00 to 18:00, on leaf, labelled
  3. MarkerA teammate at their local hour
  4. ScaleEvery three hours; every six on a phone

Responsive behaviour

The ribbon always fits the width; it never scrolls sideways. Under 768 px markers are 26 px and the “Morning” and “Evening” labels are dropped, keeping “Night” and “Working hours”. Under 576 px the scale shows every six hours. Markers within about two and a half hours of each other stack in rows, and the track grows to hold them.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A section with a hidden heading, holding a list of links. Each marker is a link named with the teammate and their local time.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves marker to marker, in order of local hour
EnterOpens the Globe with that teammate’s pin selected

Focus

The shared ring around the marker.

Labelling

Bands are named in text, not by colour alone. A teammate who hides their status outside working hours is still placed by their hour.

Contrast

Band label on leaf --color-fg-muted on --color-working-subtle
Band label on soft sun --color-fg-muted on --color-moment-subtle

Motion

A marker grows slightly when pointed at. Markers move as the clock ticks, without animation.

Touch

Markers are 26 px drawn on a phone; rows are 40 px apart so neighbours do not overlap.

Content

Band labels: “Night”, “Morning”, “Working hours”, “Evening”. The scale follows the viewer’s clock: “09:00” or “9 am”.

Code

<section class="day-ribbon" aria-labelledby="ribbon-title">
  <h2 class="visually-hidden" id="ribbon-title">The day, hour by hour</h2>
  <div class="day-ribbon__track" style="--rows: 5">
    <span class="day-ribbon__band day-ribbon__band--working" style="--from: 37.5%; --span: 37.5%"><span>Working hours</span></span>
    <ul><li><a class="day-ribbon__marker" href="/globe?pin=…" style="--at: 33.68%; --row: 0" aria-label="Tomás Herrera, 08:05"><span class="avatar hue-11" aria-hidden="true">TH</span></a></li></ul>
  </div>
</section>
Class / attributeEffect
.day-ribbon__trackThe 24-hour track; --rows sets its height.
.day-ribbon__band--night / --edge / --workingBands, placed by --from and --span.
.day-ribbon__markerA teammate; --at is the hour as a percentage, --row the row.
.day-ribbon__scaleThe hour marks.
