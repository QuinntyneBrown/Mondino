Logo

The mark, a little world with one pin on its rim, and the lowercase wordmark mondino.

Overview

The mark is a globe with one meridian and one parallel, and a single pin resting on its rim: every teammate is that pin, somewhere on the curve. The wordmark is mondino, lowercase, in Gabarito bold. In a sentence the product is “Mondino”.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. GlobeInk, with one meridian and one parallel
  2. PinThe brand’s pin colour, on the rim
  3. WordmarkLowercase, Gabarito bold, tight tracking

States

The logo is not a control. In the header it is a link, with the link’s focus ring.

Responsive behaviour

In the header the mark stands alone under 576 px and is joined by the wordmark from 576 px.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The mark is decorative (aria-hidden="true"). The wordmark is text.

Keyboard

It is not focusable and takes no keys; it is read in place by a screen reader.

Focus

As the header’s home link it takes the shared ring.

Labelling

The home link is named “Mondino home”.

Contrast

Wordmark on paper --palette-ink on --palette-paper
Wordmark on ink --palette-paper on --palette-ink
Pin on paper --palette-pin on --palette-paper

Motion

None.

Touch

As a link, its target is 44 px tall.

Content

The wordmark is never “Mondino” and never upper case. The tagline is “Your team, around the world, right now.”
