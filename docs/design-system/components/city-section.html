City section

One city’s teammates on Team, for a large team: a row with the city, its count and its local time that opens to show them.

Overview

A team of hundreds does not fit in one list. Past 40 teammates the Team screen splits its list into one city section per city, west to east: a row that names the city, says how many teammates are there and what time it is there, and opens to show them in the team table. The first two cities start open and the rest closed, so the page holds a few dozen rows however large the team is.

An open city shows at most 50 teammates; a line under them then says how many more there are and to search for them. The search above the list and the status filter both narrow what every section holds.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. ChevronPoints down when open, right when closed
  2. CityGabarito, 16 px; ends in an ellipsis if it must
  3. CountHow many teammates are in the city, with the filter and search applied
  4. Local timeThe city’s, in tabular figures, at the right
  5. RowsThe team table, without its column headings, when open

Sizes

It has one size: the row is at least 44 px tall.

States

VariantClosedOpenHoverFocus
Toggle

Responsive behaviour

The toggle is the full width of the list at every width, with the count and local time at the right and the city taking the room that is left. Under 768 px an open city’s teammates are the team table’s stacked blocks; from 768 px they are rows on the columns of the headings above the first city, and each city’s own headings are hidden from view.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

WAI-ARIA Disclosure: a <button aria-expanded> inside an <h3>, controlling the table beneath it with aria-controls. Each city is a <section>, and its table is captioned with the city: “Teammates in Honolulu”.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabCity to city; into an open city’s teammates on the way
Enter / SpaceOpens or closes the city

Focus

The shared ring, inside the row. Opening or closing a city leaves focus on its button.

Labelling

The button’s name is the city, its count and its local time: “San Francisco 27 teammates 07:05”. The word “teammates” is hidden text after the number. Open or closed is carried by aria-expanded as well as the chevron.

Contrast

City and local time --color-fg-default on --color-bg-canvas
Count --color-fg-muted on --color-bg-canvas
City, on hover --color-fg-default on --color-bg-surface
Chevron --color-fg-muted on --color-bg-canvas

Motion

The chevron turns over --duration-fast, and the row’s background changes with it. The rows appear at once. Reduced motion sets every duration token to nothing.

Touch

The whole row is the target, at least 44 px tall (--target-comfortable).

Content

The city’s name as the teammates’ pins give it, with no country: “Honolulu”, “San Francisco”. The count is a bare number. The local time follows the viewer’s clock, 24-hour or 12-hour. Cities run west to east, like the table. The search that narrows them is named “Find a person or city”. Under a city’s 50th teammate the line reads “14 more teammates in London.”, the count and the city in bold, then “Search to find them.”
