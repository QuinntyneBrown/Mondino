Empty state

What a screen says when it has nothing to show, could not load, or is not for this person.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

One icon, one title, one sentence and at most one main action. It is used when a list has nothing in it, when a screen’s data could not be loaded, and for the pages that say one thing: you have left, you need an invite link, this address leads nowhere. See empty and error states.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. IconOn a soft disc; danger-tinted for an error
  2. TitleWhat is true, plainly
  3. TextWhy, and what is unharmed
  4. ActionOne way forward

Sizes

In a list it is compact. As a whole page it fills the space under the header, centred, with the title at heading size.

You need an invite link

Mondino shows a team to its own teammates. Ask a teammate for their team’s invite link and open it here to put yourself on the map.

States

An empty state is not a control. Its button has the states of a button.

Responsive behaviour

Centred, at most --layout-container-message wide. On Join it is left-aligned in the form’s column. Its buttons wrap and stay centred.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain content. Where it is the whole page, its title is the page’s <h1>. A filter’s one-line note is role="status".

Keyboard

It takes no keys; its button or link does.

Focus

The header stays, so every other screen is still one Tab away.

Labelling

The icon is decorative; the title carries the meaning.

Contrast

Title --color-fg-default on --color-bg-canvas
Text --color-fg-muted on --color-bg-canvas
Error icon --color-danger-fg on --color-danger-bg

Motion

None.

Touch

The target is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-comfortable).

Content

Nothing yet: what will appear here and when. Could not load: “We couldn’t load…”, that nothing is lost, and “Try again”. A filter that found nothing says so in the list’s own words: “Everyone is awake right now.”
