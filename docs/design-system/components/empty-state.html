Empty state

What a screen says when it has nothing to show, when a filter or a search found nothing, or when it could not load.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

One icon, one title, one sentence and one or two ways on. It is used when a list has nothing in it and when a screen’s data could not be loaded. A filter or a search that found nothing gets a smaller form in the list’s own place, with a button that clears it. A team of one, on Team, gets a card that asks for one thing: invite teammates. See empty and error states.

The pages that say one thing (you have left, you need an invite link, this address leads nowhere) are a component of their own, the notice page; a notice that takes a form’s place on Join is part of auth.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. IconOn a soft disc; danger-tinted for an error
  2. TitleWhat is true, plainly
  3. TextWhy, and what is unharmed
  4. Actions“Try again” first; a second way on where there is one

Sizes

In a list it is compact, with the title at --text-h2. As a whole screen under the header (.message-page) it is centred in the space, with the title at --text-h1 and the text at body size. The no-results form is smaller again: a title at --text-h3 over one line.

States

An empty state is not a control. Its button has the states of a button.

Responsive behaviour

Centred, at most --layout-container-message wide. Its buttons wrap and stay centred. The card for a team of one stacks its text over its button under 576 px and puts the button at the right from 576 px.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain content. Where it is the whole screen, its title is the page’s <h1>. The no-results form is role="status", so a filter or a search that empties the list is announced. The card for a team of one is headed by an <h2>.

Keyboard

It takes no keys; its buttons and links do. “Show everyone” and “Show everything” clear the filter and the search and return focus to the list.

Focus

The header stays, so every other screen is still one Tab away.

Labelling

The icon is decorative; the title carries the meaning.

Contrast

Title --color-fg-default on --color-bg-canvas
Text --color-fg-muted on --color-bg-canvas
Error icon --color-danger-fg on --color-danger-bg
No-results title --color-fg-default on --color-bg-canvas
Note in the panel --color-fg-muted on --color-bg-canvas

Motion

None.

Touch

The target is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-comfortable).

Content

Nothing yet: what will appear here and when: “Nothing coming up” / “No holidays, races or local events in the next 90 days where your teammates live. We’ll add them here as they’re announced.” Could not load: what did not load, that nothing is lost, and “Try again”: “The globe didn’t load”, “The team didn’t load”, “Moments didn’t load”, “Your settings didn’t load”.

A filter or search that found nothing says so in the list’s own words and offers the way back: “No one is asleep right now” / “Try another filter.” / “Show everyone”; “No sport moments coming up” / “None of your teammates’ cities have one in the next 90 days.” / “Show everything”; “No one matches “Reykjavik”” / “Try another filter.” / “Show everyone”. What was typed is quoted as text.

A team of one: “It’s just you on Northwind so far” / “This list fills up west to east as teammates join with the invite link.” / “Invite teammates”.
