Globe

The little world itself: every teammate’s pin on it, its night side shaded, in colours of its own in light and in dark.

Overview

The globe is the home of the product. In the product it is a 3D model built in Blender, lit and shaded, with the soft, rounded, toy-like look of an animated film; these pages and the mocks draw it still, in the same colours. It turns slowly on its own, turns to a selected pin and holds there, and is dragged to spin.

The globe is a picture with a text equivalent: a labelled group holding a list of pins, each named in full. Which renderer draws it is decided in an ADR.

Its parts, in reading order:

  2. LandGreen, with sand, forest and ice

Sizes

The globe fills the smaller of its container’s width and height. Under 400 px wide its pins are drawn smaller and only a lifted pin carries a label. On a page that says one thing it is a small picture, --layout-notice-art square, with no pins.

Responsive behaviour

Phone: a square the width of the screen, under the heading and above the panel. From 576 px the stage is 4:3 with the heading and legend laid over its corners. From 992 px it fills the height beside the panel, and from 1200 px it keeps growing while text keeps its measure. A vertical drag that the globe cannot follow scrolls the page.

The legend shows from 576 px. Where the globe is a still picture, a phone has no legend to say so, and a line under the globe (.globe-screen__still) says it instead; it is hidden from 576 px, where the legend carries the same words.

Theming

The globe’s colours are tokens of their own (--color-globe-*). In dark it is the same picture a little deeper: a deeper ocean and land, ice and cloud a step off white, a darker dusk on its night side, and a light outline in place of the ink one. Both sets are the prototype’s own. It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A role="group" named for what it holds (“The globe, with 13 teammates’ pins. Drag to spin, or use the arrow keys.”), containing a list of pins. The 3D canvas itself is hidden from assistive technology. As a still picture the group is named “A still picture of the globe, with 13 teammates’ pins.” and is not focusable, since it cannot be turned; its pins are the same buttons. On Join, Sign In, Create Team, Profile & Settings and a page that says one thing it is a single role="img".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the globe, then to each pin in view
← / → / ↑ / ↓With focus on the globe, turns it by a fixed step

EscClears the selection

Focus

The shared ring on the globe’s box and on each pin. Selecting a pin by keyboard moves focus to the panel’s heading; closing returns it to the pin.

Labelling

Nothing is available only as a picture: every pin is a named button, and the panel and Team list everyone. That holds for the still picture too: its pins lift and open the panel.

Contrast

The outline against the page: ink in light, a light line in dark --color-globe-outline on --color-bg-canvas
The ocean against the page --color-globe-ocean on --color-bg-canvas
Pin label on day and night --color-pin-label-fg on --color-pin-label-bg

Motion

It turns on its own, and animates to a selected pin. Under reduced motion it does neither: it shows the pin facing the viewer at once. It neither turns nor draws while its tab is hidden.

Touch

One finger spins it and one tap selects a pin. Nothing needs two fingers.

Content

The legend names “Night side”, “Moment this week”, “Teammates close together” and “Drag to spin”, from 576 px. With every kind of moment switched off it drops “Moment this week”. Over a still picture its last entry reads “Still view — 3D isn’t available in this browser” in place of “Drag to spin”, and on a phone the line under the globe adds “Pins still open.” The heading gives the count: “13 teammates”, then “12 time zones · 8 working · 2 asleep”; for a large team, “500 teammates” and “38 time zones · 324 working · 42 asleep”.
