Panel row

A line in the panel that selects a teammate: a moment this week, or someone in working hours now.

Overview

The panel beside the globe is made of rows. With no pin selected it lists “This week around the world” and “In working hours now”. Choosing a row selects that teammate’s pin and turns the globe to it.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. LeadAn emblem, or an avatar
  2. TitleThe moment, or the teammate
  3. MetaWho and where
  4. TrailWhen, or the local time

Responsive behaviour

Rows are the width of the panel: the screen’s width under the globe below 992 px, --layout-panel-width beside it from 992 px. Titles and names truncate with an ellipsis; the trail never wraps.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A link in a list. The list sits in a section with a heading (“This week around the world”).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves row to row
EnterSelects the row’s teammate and opens their panel

Focus

The shared ring around the whole row. After choosing, focus moves to the teammate’s name in the panel.

Labelling

A moment’s kind is read before the teammate, as hidden text, since the emblem alone shows it.

Contrast

Title --color-fg-default on --color-bg-surface
Meta and trail --color-fg-muted on --color-bg-surface

Motion

Colour and size changes take --duration-fast. Reduced motion sets every duration token to nothing, so the change is instant with no extra rule.

Touch

The target is at least 44 × 44 px (--target-comfortable).

Content

Moments run from today to 7 days ahead, in date order, at most eight, then “All moments”. Working teammates are ordered by local hour. With no one working: “Nobody — it’s quiet everywhere.”
