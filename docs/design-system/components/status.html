Status

Where a teammate is in their day, in words: Working hours, Early morning, Evening, Probably asleep.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

Status comes only from the teammate’s local hour, never from what they do. It is shown as a badge in the panel, as words under a name in the Team table, and as a dot beside a local time. A teammate who has turned off “Share whether I’m working or asleep” has no status at any hour: no badge, no words, no dot, no working-hours overlap. They are counted neither as working nor as asleep, and are not listed under “In working hours now”, Working now or Asleep.

Sizes

It has one size.

States

A status is not a control. Its four values are shown under Variants.

Responsive behaviour

The badge and the words are the same at every width. Under 768 px the Team table keeps the words under the name, so a status is never a dot alone.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain text. The dot is decorative and hidden from assistive technology.

Keyboard

It is not focusable and takes no keys; it is read in place by a screen reader.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

The status is always given as its words. When the hour turns, the words change without a reload and without an announcement. Where a teammate does not share it, nothing stands in its place; the panel’s private note says why.

Contrast

Working hours --color-fg-working on --color-working-subtle
Early morning, Evening --color-fg-default on --color-moment-subtle
Probably asleep --color-fg-on-asleep on --color-asleep-bg

Motion

None.

Touch

Not a control.

Content

The four labels are fixed: “Working hours”, “Early morning”, “Evening”, “Probably asleep”. “Probably” is deliberate: Mondino knows the hour, not the person.
