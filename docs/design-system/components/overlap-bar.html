Overlap bar

The part of your working day and a teammate’s that coincides, drawn on your own clock.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

Working hours are 09:00 to 18:00 local for everyone. The bar is a 24-hour track on the viewer’s clock: their own working day outlined, the teammate’s filled faintly, the shared part filled solid, and a tick for now. It appears in a teammate’s panel, never in your own, and not for a teammate who has turned off “Share whether I’m working or asleep”: their panel has no overlap at all.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. Title“Working-hours overlap”
  2. The overlap in figures“16:00–18:00 your time”, or “None today”
  3. Your working dayAn ink outline, 09:00–18:00
  4. BothSolid pin
  5. TheirsFaint pin
  6. NowAn ink tick
  7. The overlap in wordsA sentence with start, end and length

Sizes

It has one size.

States

The bar is not a control. It redraws when the clock passes an hour.

Responsive behaviour

The track is the full width of the panel at every width. Its scale shows five marks: midnight, 06:00, 12:00, 18:00, midnight.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A section with a heading. The track, scale and legend are decorative; the sentence under them is the bar’s text equivalent.

Keyboard

It is not focusable and takes no keys; it is read in place by a screen reader.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

The sentence gives start, end and length: “You and Tomás share 2 hours of working hours: 16:00 to 18:00 on your clock.”

Contrast

Both, on the track --color-people on --color-bg-canvas
Your outline --color-fg-default on --color-bg-canvas
Sentence --color-fg-muted on --color-bg-surface

Motion

None.

Touch

Not a control.

Content

Times follow the viewer’s clock, 24-hour or 12-hour. With nothing shared the figure reads “None today” and the sentence gives the teammate’s working day on your clock.
