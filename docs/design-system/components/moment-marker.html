Moment marker

The small sun disc beside a pin that says something is happening there this week: the kind’s icon for one moment, a count for two or more.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

A pin whose teammate has a moment today or in the next 7 days carries a marker. With one moment this week the marker shows the icon of its kind; with two or more it shows how many (“2”). It counts only the kinds the viewer has left on in Profile & Settings. A marker for a moment today is larger and ringed.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. Emblem or countThe kind’s icon, 10 px, ink; or the number of moments this week, 10 px Gabarito
  2. DiscSun; 16 px, or 20 px with an ink ring when the moment is today; a count widens it into a pill

Variants

LOLOLOLOLOTH2
The four kinds, this week; today; and a count, when a pin has two or more moments this week

Sizes

The size carries meaning: 16 px for a moment this week, 20 px with a ring for one today. A count keeps the height and grows wider with its digits. It is never drawn smaller than 16 px.

States

A marker is not a control; it lifts with its pin.

Responsive behaviour

On a globe under 500 px the marker keeps its 16 px and moves further out so it does not cover the small pin.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Decorative. Its moment is in the pin’s accessible name (“October Bank Holiday, today”) and in the lifted label as text. A count’s moments are all in the name: “2 moments this week: Día de Muertos, in 5 days; Clásico Nacional, in 6 days”.

Keyboard

It is not focusable and takes no keys; it is read in place by a screen reader.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

Today’s marker is stronger by size and a ring, not by colour. A count is a number, never a second colour.

Contrast

Emblem --moment-marker-fg on --moment-marker-bg
Count --moment-marker-fg on --moment-marker-bg

Motion

None of its own.

Touch

Not a control; the pin is.

Content

When its pin is lifted, the label gives the title and when: “Today”, “Tomorrow”, “In 3 days”. A count is a bare number with no plus sign and no word.

<button class="pin hue-11" type="button" aria-pressed="false" aria-label="Tomás Herrera, Mexico City, 08:05, Early morning, Partly cloudy 22°, 2 moments this week: Día de Muertos, in 5 days; Clásico Nacional, in 6 days"><span aria-hidden="true">TH</span><span class="moment-marker moment-marker--count" aria-hidden="true">2</span></button>
Class / attributeEffect
.moment-markerA moment in the next 7 days.
.moment-marker--todayA moment today: larger, with an ink ring.
.moment-marker--countTwo or more moments this week: the count in place of the icon.
