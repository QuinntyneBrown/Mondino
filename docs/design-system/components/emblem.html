Emblem

A moment’s icon on a disc of sun: what kind of moment it is, at a glance.

Overview

An emblem marks a moment in the panel, on a moment card, on today’s moment and beside a switch. Its small form on the globe is the moment marker. It shows Mondino’s own icon for the kind; a team’s or a series’ own logo is used only where Mondino holds the right to it.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. Icon16 px, ink
  2. Disc36 px; sun, or soft sun

Variants

The four kinds on soft sun; solid for today or a card; plain when a kind is switched off

Sizes

32 px beside a switch, 36 px in a list, 56 px on today’s moment

States

An emblem is not a control.

Responsive behaviour

The same sizes at every width.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Decorative beside the kind’s name. Where the kind is not written beside it, the icon carries the kind as its label.

Keyboard

It is not focusable and takes no keys; it is read in place by a screen reader.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

The kind is always available as text: written, or as the icon’s aria-label.

Contrast

Icon on sun --color-fg-on-moment on --color-moment
Icon on soft sun --color-fg-default on --color-moment-subtle

Motion

None.

Touch

Not a control.

Content

One icon per kind, always the same: a party popper for a holiday, a flag for Formula 1, a ticket for a local event, a trophy for sport.
