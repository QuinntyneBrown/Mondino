Avatar

A teammate’s initials in their own colour, wherever they appear off the globe.

Overview

An avatar stands beside a name in the panel, the Team table, the day ribbon and a moment. On the globe the same initials and colour are a pin. Each teammate is given one of twelve colours when they join.

Anatomy

  1. InitialsThe first character of up to the first two words; Gabarito semibold, white
  2. DiscThe teammate’s colour

States

An avatar is not a control and has one state. A row or a marker that holds it carries the hover and focus.

Responsive behaviour

On the day ribbon avatars are 26 px under 768 px and 32 px from 768 px. Elsewhere the size is fixed.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Decorative: aria-hidden="true", because the name always stands beside it. Where it stands alone, as on the day ribbon, the link around it carries the name.

Keyboard

It is not focusable and takes no keys; it is read in place by a screen reader.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

Never the only carrier of who someone is.

Contrast

Initials on colour 1 --color-on-teammate on --palette-teammate-1
Initials on colour 11 --color-on-teammate on --palette-teammate-11

Motion

None.

Touch

Not a control.

Content

Initials are the first character of up to the first two words of the name, upper case. A one-word name gives one initial; an avatar is never blank.
