Team table

Everyone, west to east: each teammate’s location, local time, offset from you, weather and next moment; for a large team, a search and a section per city.

Overview

The table on the Team screen lists every teammate ordered by their time zone’s current offset from UTC, then by longitude. A row opens the Globe with that teammate’s pin selected. It is filtered by a segmented control: All, Working now, Asleep. A teammate who does not share whether they are working or asleep has no status under their name and is in neither Working now nor Asleep.

A large team gets two more things above and around the table: a search, “Find a person or city”, that matches a name or a city within the status filter, and one city section per city, west to east. The column headings are then shown once, above the first city (.team-head), and each city’s own table keeps its headings for assistive technology only.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. AvatarDecorative
  2. NameThe row’s link; “(you)” on your own
  3. StatusIn words, under the name
  4. City and countryNever a postal code
  5. Local timeGabarito, tabular, with a status dot
  6. Offset“7h behind”, under “Compared to you”; a dash for you
  7. Weather and next momentIcon and degrees; a moment pill or a dash

Responsive behaviour

Under 768 px it is not a grid of columns: each teammate is a block with the name and status at the left, the local time with city and offset at the right, and the next moment on a line beneath. From 768 px it is a table with all six columns, and between 768 and 992 px the columns tighten and a moment pill may wrap. From 992 px a chevron ends each row.

For a large team the search takes the full width above the filter under 576 px and is --layout-search-width wide beside it from 576 px; the filter scrolls sideways inside its own box if the two cannot share a row. The shared column headings (.team-head) show from 768 px, on the same columns as the tables beneath them. The rows held in the page stay bounded: the first two cities start open, and an open city shows at most 50 teammates.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A real <table> with a hidden caption, column headers with scope="col", and the name as each row’s header. The name is the row’s one link; its target covers the row. In a large team each city is its own table, captioned “Teammates in Honolulu”, with its column headers kept for assistive technology and hidden from view; the visible headings above the first city are aria-hidden. The search is a type="search" input named “Find a person or city” that controls the list.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves row to row, by name
EnterOpens the Globe with that teammate’s pin selected

Focus

The ring is drawn around the whole row.

Labelling

Status is given in words. A weather icon carries the condition as its label. A dash has a label: “You”, “No moment coming up”.

Contrast

Name --color-fg-default on --color-bg-canvas
Status and offset --color-fg-muted on --color-bg-canvas
Row hover --color-fg-default on --color-bg-surface

Motion

Colour and size changes take --duration-fast. Reduced motion sets every duration token to nothing, so the change is instant with no extra rule.

Touch

The whole row is the target.

Content

Column headers: “Person”, “Location”, “Local time”, “Compared to you”, “Weather”, “Coming up”. An offset reads “7h behind”, “4h 30m ahead” or “Same time”; your own row has a dash. The search’s placeholder and name are “Find a person or city”. A name is data: it is shown as the characters that were typed, never as markup.
