Pin

A teammate on the globe: their initials in their own colour.

Overview

A pin is a person, their clock and their sky. The pin itself carries the initials; the pin label beside it carries the local time and weather; a moment marker says something is happening there this week. A pin is placed at the teammate’s city, never at their postal code.

A pin that is pointed at, focused or selected lifts out from the globe: it grows, rises, comes in front of its neighbours and shows its full label.

Teammates whose pins would cover each other are drawn as one group pin with a count, at every globe size: two teammates in one city, or sixty across three. Selecting it turns the globe to it and lists its teammates by city in the panel; choosing one selects that teammate’s pin, and a selected teammate’s pin always stands alone.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. Initials11 px Gabarito semibold, white
  2. Moment marker (optional)Sun, top right
  3. Disc and ringThe teammate’s colour; a white ring, or ink for you, or pin when selected

Variants

A teammate; you (an ink ring); with today’s moment; a group pin (two teammates in one city, and 68 across three cities); a nameless pin on Join; your pin on Join

Sizes

18 px on a globe under 400 px, 26 px at rest, 34 px lifted. The hit area is 44 px at every size.

States

Pointed at, focused and selected all lift the pin. Selected adds a pin-coloured ring and stays lifted. A newly placed pin pulses once.

Responsive behaviour

On a globe under 400 px wide pins are 18 px with no initials, and only a lifted pin is labelled. At every globe size, pins closer than about 40 px merge into one group pin with a count; a pin alone keeps its label. Pins are never fanned out around their place.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <button aria-pressed> in the globe’s list of pins. Its name is the whole teammate: name, city, local time, status, weather, and any moment this week. A group pin is the same button, named with its count and who or where it holds: “2 teammates close together: Sofia Bianchi (you), Luca Ferri”, “14 teammates close together, Mexico City”, “68 teammates close together, 3 cities”.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the pin; it lifts
Enter / SpaceSelects it; the panel opens on the teammate, or on a group pin’s teammates by city
EscClears the selection; the pin settles back

Focus

The shared ring around the lifted pin.

Labelling

Your own pin is marked “(you)” in its name and by an ink ring: more than colour. A selected pin is told apart by its size, its lift, its opened label and aria-pressed; its pin-coloured ring is only 1.4:1 against the ocean and is never the only sign.

Contrast

Initials --color-on-teammate on --palette-teammate-11
Lifted label beside a selected pin --palette-paper on --palette-ink
Count on a group pin --color-fg-on-accent on --color-accent

Motion

Lifts within 200 ms. A new pin pulses once over --duration-deliberate. Under reduced motion it changes size with no rise, and does not pulse.

Touch

The hit area is 44 × 44 px whatever the pin’s drawn size. A tap selects; a tap elsewhere on the globe clears.

Content

Initials are the first character of up to the first two words of the name. A pin is never blank. A group pin shows its count as a bare number.
