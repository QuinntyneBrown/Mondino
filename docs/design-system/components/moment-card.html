Moment card

A moment on soft sun, in a teammate’s panel; and its one-line form, the pill, in the Team table.

Overview

A moment is something happening where a teammate lives: a holiday, Formula 1, a local event or sport. In a teammate’s panel each upcoming moment is a card with its kind, when, title and one line of detail. In the Team table the next moment is a pill. In the Moments list it is a moment row.

Anatomy

Its parts, in reading order:

  1. EmblemThe kind’s icon on sun
  2. Kind and when“Holiday · In 5 days”
  3. TitleGabarito, 16 px; at most 80 characters
  4. DetailOne line; at most 160 characters

Sizes

It has one size.

States

A card and a pill are not controls.

Responsive behaviour

A card is the width of the panel. A pill truncates its title on one line; between 768 and 992 px, where the column is narrow, it wraps to two.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so light and dark both come from tokens.css. To re-theme it, override the tokens listed under Tokens at :root or in a [data-theme] block; never write a colour in the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain content. In a panel the title is a heading under the teammate’s name.

Keyboard

It is not focusable and takes no keys; it is read in place by a screen reader.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

The kind is written on a card. On a pill the icon carries it as its label.

Contrast

Title on soft sun --color-fg-default on --color-moment-subtle
Kind and detail --color-fg-muted on --color-moment-subtle

Motion

None.

Touch

Not a control.

Content

A title and a detail from an outside calendar are text, never HTML, and are cut with an ellipsis at 80 and 160 characters. “When” is “Today”, “Tomorrow”, “In 3 days” up to 7 days, then the date.
