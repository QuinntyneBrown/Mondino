Pattern

Forms

Mondino has four forms: joining a team, signing in, making a team, and your own settings. All ask for very little and lose nothing.

Overview

Join has exactly three fields: your name, a country, a postal code. Sign In has one, a work email. Create Team has five: the team’s name, your name, a work email, a country and a postal code. Profile & Settings has a country and postal code, two choices, and six switches. None asks for an address, a password or a phone number, and an email is asked for only where a sign-in link has to be sent. Join, Sign In and Create Team share one layout, auth.

When to use

  • Submit at once (Join, Sign In, Create Team): one screen, one button whose label says what happens next. On Join it follows the form: “Place my pin”, then “Join Northwind” once the city is found.
  • Change, then save (Profile & Settings): nothing applies until “Save changes” in the save bar, which appears when anything differs from what was saved. A new postal code is looked up with “Look up”, and the pin moves only on saving.
  • A notice instead of a form: when the form cannot be used (the invite link no longer works, the team is full) or has done its work (“Check your inbox”), a notice takes its place.
  • There are no inline forms: editing opened by a button is a dialog or a screen.

Structure

Milan, Lombardy — pinned at city level. 13° and foggy right now.

Join: label above each field, the lookup message under the postal code, one block button
  • Labels sit above their fields. Two short related fields share a row from 576 px.
  • On Join and Create Team the postal code is looked up when the teammate leaves the field or submits. On Profile & Settings it is looked up from “Look up”, beside the field. The result takes the place of the privacy line.
  • Changing the postal code or the country removes a found city and its pin.
  • Under the form, one line offers the other way in: “Already on Mondino? Sign in”.

Responsive behaviour

One column under 576 px. From 576 px country and postal code share a row, and “Look up” joins them at the end of it on Profile & Settings. On Join, Sign In and Create Team the form is above the globe under 992 px and beside it from 992 px.

Accessibility

  • Every control has a visible <label>. Help and errors are tied with aria-describedby; an invalid control has aria-invalid="true".
  • The lookup’s result is announced through a polite live region; its error is an alert.
  • On submit with errors, focus moves to the summary, or to the one invalid field.
  • When a notice or a finished screen replaces the form, focus moves to its title.
  • A switch is role="switch" with aria-checked; a two-way choice is a radio group. “Your city” is a combobox.
  • Leaving Profile & Settings with unsaved changes asks first.

Content

Labels are nouns. The button is the next thing that will happen. An error starts with what to do. See Content.
