Pattern

Forms

Mondino has two forms: joining a team, and your own settings. Both ask for very little and lose nothing.

Overview

Join has exactly three fields: your name, a country, a postal code. Profile & Settings has a country and postal code, two choices, and six switches. Neither asks for an address, an e-mail or a phone number.

When to use

  • Submit at once (Join): one screen, one button whose label follows the form: “Place my pin”, then “Join Northwind” once the city is found.
  • Change, then save (Profile & Settings): nothing applies until “Save changes” in the save bar, which appears when anything differs from what was saved.
  • There are no inline forms: editing opened by a button is a dialog or a screen.

Structure

Milan, Lombardy — pinned at city level. 13° and foggy right now.

Join: label above each field, the lookup message under the postal code, one block button
  • Labels sit above their fields. Two short related fields share a row from 576 px.
  • The postal code is looked up when the teammate leaves the field or submits. The result takes the place of the privacy line.
  • Changing the postal code or the country removes a found city and its pin.

States

InvalidA summary at the top with a link to each field; each field marked and explained

FailedAn alert above the button; everything typed is intact
Rate limited“Wait a moment, then try again.”; what was typed is kept

Responsive behaviour

One column under 576 px. From 576 px country and postal code share a row. On Join the form is above the globe under 992 px and beside it from 992 px.

Accessibility

  • Every control has a visible <label>. Help and errors are tied with aria-describedby; an invalid control has aria-invalid="true".
  • The lookup’s result is announced through a polite live region; its error is an alert.
  • On submit with errors, focus moves to the summary.
  • A switch is role="switch" with aria-checked; a two-way choice is a radio group.
  • Leaving Profile & Settings with unsaved changes asks first.

Content

Labels are nouns. The button is the next thing that will happen. An error starts with what to do. See Content.
