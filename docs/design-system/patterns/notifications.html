Pattern

Notifications

Mondino sends one thing, the sign-in link a teammate asks for. Everything else it tells you on the screen you are looking at.

Overview

There is no notification centre, no feed, no push and no badge. A teammate opens Mondino when they want to know what it is like where someone is. The only notices are answers to their own actions, and one about the screen’s freshness. The one email Mondino sends is the sign-in link a teammate asks for on Sign In; it works once, for 15 minutes.

Structure

Couldn’t copy the link. It’s selected — press Ctrl+C or ⌘C to copy it yourself.

Toasts: two successes, and a failure

States

One notice of a kind at a time. A new toast replaces the last. A banner and the save bar can be present together, as they sit at opposite ends of the screen.

Responsive behaviour

A toast and the save bar: bottom centre, 16 px from the sides on a phone. A banner: the full width under the header, its button wrapping under its text.

Accessibility

  • The toast region is in the page before any toast, so a screen reader hears it arrive.
  • Success is polite (role="status"); a failure is an alert, and differs by its surface, edge, icon and words, not by colour alone.
  • Nothing dismisses itself while it is pointed at or focused, and a failure never does.
  • The clocks ticking each minute are never announced.

Content

What happened in a few words, then the next step: “Invite link copied. Paste it wherever your team talks.”; “Couldn’t copy the link. It’s selected — press Ctrl+C or ⌘C to copy it yourself.”; “You’re on the map. Northwind can now see your pin in Milan.” For the banner: what is still live (the clocks) and what is stale (weather and moments, and since when): “Couldn’t refresh. Clocks are live; weather and moments are from 14:50.” Its button is “Try again”, and “Trying…” meanwhile.
