Content and tone

A small, warm product about people far apart. It speaks plainly and never claims to know more than a clock and a calendar can tell.

Overview

The rules of wording are on Content. This page is about tone: how the words should feel on each kind of screen.

States

The tone holds in every state. A loading message names the thing. An error does not apologise at length or joke. A page for someone outside a team is kind and gives nothing away.

Responsive behaviour

Copy is written to fit a phone first: a heading in one or two lines at 320 px, a button label in one.

Accessibility

  • Plain words, short sentences, no idiom that does not travel: the team is spread around the world and reads in a second language.
  • Nothing is said by an emoji or an icon alone.
  • A name is shown exactly as its teammate typed it.

Content

Mondino has no chat, no calendar, no presence and no feed, and its words must not suggest any: nothing is “online”, “active”, “available” or “busy”, and nothing is “scheduled”.
