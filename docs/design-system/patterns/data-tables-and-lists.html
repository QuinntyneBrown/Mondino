Pattern

Data tables and lists

How Mondino lists teammates and moments: in an order that means something, with a row that opens the globe, at any size of team.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Mondino has three lists: the panel’s rows, the Team table, and the Moments list. None is sorted by the viewer. Each has one fixed order that answers a question. Only a large team’s Team list is searched, and it is then split into a section per city.

States

  • Loading: skeleton rows in the shape of real ones.
  • A filter or a search with nothing: a title, a line and a button that clears it; the filter and its counts stay.
  • Counts are live: when the clock passes an hour that changes a status, the rows and the counts change with no reload. A teammate who does not share whether they are working or asleep is counted in neither.
  • A value is missing: a dash, with a label for a screen reader. Weather unavailable says so in its cell.
  • Long: a team may hold 500. Past 40 teammates the day ribbon becomes a bar chart, the list gains a search and splits into a section per city, and the globe merges close pins into group pins with counts. The rows held in the page stay bounded: two cities open to begin with, and at most 50 teammates shown in a city.

Responsive behaviour

The Team table is a table from 768 px and a stack of blocks under it; it never scrolls sideways. A large team’s search is the full width above the filter under 576 px and beside it from there. Only the day ribbon’s bar chart scrolls sideways on a phone, inside its own box. Moment rows move whose it is under the text on a phone. Panel rows truncate.

Accessibility

  • The Team table is a real table with a caption and header cells; the name is each row’s header and its one link. In a large team each city is its own captioned table under a heading with a disclosure button.
  • The Team filter is a tab list; arrow keys move between its tabs. The Moments filter is a group of pressed buttons. The search is a labelled search field that controls the list.
  • A group heading with nothing under it is not rendered.
  • Times, offsets and temperatures use tabular figures.

Content

A row leads with who or what, then where, then when. Another teammate’s row never shows a postal code: city and country only.
