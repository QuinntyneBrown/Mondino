Pattern

Data tables and lists

How Mondino lists teammates and moments: in an order that means something, with a row that opens the globe.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Mondino has three lists: the panel’s rows, the Team table, and the Moments list. None is sorted by the viewer and none is searched. Each has one fixed order that answers a question.

Structure

Everyone, west to east

A heading, a filter with counts, then the rows.

The head of a list

States

  • Loading: skeleton rows in the shape of real ones.
  • A filter with nothing: one line; the filter and its counts stay.
  • Counts are live: when the clock passes an hour that changes a status, the rows and the counts change with no reload.
  • A value is missing: a dash, with a label for a screen reader. Weather unavailable says so in its cell.
  • Long: a team may hold 500. The rows held in the page stay under a fixed limit as the list scrolls. How the day ribbon and the globe draw hundreds is not designed yet.

Responsive behaviour

The Team table is a table from 768 px and a stack of blocks under it; it never scrolls sideways. Moment rows move whose it is under the text on a phone. Panel rows truncate.

Accessibility

  • The Team table is a real table with a caption and header cells; the name is each row’s header and its one link.
  • The Team filter is a tab list; arrow keys move between its tabs. The Moments filter is a group of pressed buttons.
  • A group heading with nothing under it is not rendered.
  • Times, offsets and temperatures use tabular figures.

Content

A row leads with who or what, then where, then when. Another teammate’s row never shows a postal code: city and country only.
