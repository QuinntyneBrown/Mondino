Pattern

Feedback and loading

Every action answers at once, where the teammate is looking, and waiting always says what it is waiting for.

Overview

Mondino tells you what happened in the place it happened. It has four ways to do so and uses the quietest that works.

When to use

UseWhenExample
The control itselfAn action is in flightA busy button: “Joining Northwind…”
A message in placeSomething about a field or a sectionThe lookup message; a field error
AlertSomething failed or needs a decision, and stays until resolvedA join that failed; a full team
Save barSettings differ from what was savedUnsaved, saving, saved
ToastAn action worked and left no other traceInvite link copied
BannerSomething affects the whole screenCould not refresh
DialogA destructive action, or work about to be lostLeave Northwind?
SkeletonA screen’s first data is loadingGlobe, Team, Moments

Structure

Finding 20121…

A busy button, a lookup in flight, and a skeleton row

States

  • Under about 300 ms: show nothing. A flash is worse than a pause.
  • First load: skeletons in the shape of what is coming; the globe’s place holds a plain disc; nothing shifts when it arrives. The heading, counts and panel are usable before the 3D globe is.
  • Refresh: quiet. Every 15 minutes while visible, the team and weather are read again and the screen updates without losing the selection or the globe’s position. A hidden tab does no work.
  • Could not refresh: the screen keeps its last data, the clocks keep ticking, and a banner says so and that Mondino will try again.
  • A source failed: everything that does not depend on it is still shown. Weather’s place says it is unavailable; it is never shown as zero.

Responsive behaviour

A toast and the save bar sit at the bottom centre at every width. A banner spans the screen under the header.

Accessibility

  • A loading region has aria-busy="true" and one hidden status message.
  • Success is announced politely (role="status"); a failure that needs the teammate now is an alert.
  • Feedback never moves focus, except a form’s error summary.
  • A success that dismisses itself waits while it is pointed at or focused. A failure never dismisses itself.

Content

In flight: the verb in progress with an ellipsis. Done: what happened and what is now true: “Saved — your pin is up to date”. Failed: what happened, that nothing is lost, and “Try again”.
