Pattern

Dialogs and overlays

Mondino interrupts three times only. Everything else happens in place.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

A dialog stops everything, so it is kept for the three moments when stopping is right: when a teammate asks to invite someone and needs the team’s invite link in front of them, before a team is left, and before unsaved settings are lost. The first is opened on purpose and closes freely; the other two are questions that must be answered.

Structure

Invite teammates to Northwind

Share this link with anyone on the team. They place their own pin with a postal code — only their city is ever shown.

13 on the team · link never expires
Asked for: a title, a close button, what the link does, the link in a copy field, and a foot

Leave without saving?

You changed your settings but haven’t saved them. If you leave now, those changes are lost and your pin stays as it was.

Discard and leaveKeep editing
A question: what is lost, the other choice and the safe one

States

  • Open: the page behind is inert and dimmed. Focus is on the safe action of a question (“Stay”, “Keep editing”), or on “Copy link” in the invite dialog.
  • Busy: the confirming button is busy (“Leaving…”); “Stay” is disabled; nothing can be sent twice.
  • Failed: an alert at the top of the dialog; nothing has changed; “Try again”.
  • Copied: the invite dialog closes and a toast says “Invite link copied. Paste it wherever your team talks.”
  • Copying was refused: the invite dialog stays open with the link selected and focused, and a toast that stays says which keys to press.
  • Done: leaving goes to a page that confirms it. Only one dialog is ever open.

Responsive behaviour

Under 576 px a dialog is a sheet from the bottom edge with stacked, full-width buttons, the confirming action on top. From 576 px it is centred with its buttons in a row.

Accessibility

  • The invite dialog is role="dialog"; the two questions are role="alertdialog". All are aria-modal="true", named by their title and described by their body.
  • Focus is kept inside. Escape closes it and returns focus to what opened it; so do “Close”, “Stay” and “Keep editing”. Nothing has changed.
  • Built on CDK Dialog. Never hand-roll a modal.
  • Layers come from tokens: backdrop, dialog, toast. A toast sits over an open dialog.

Content

A question’s title names the thing: “Leave Northwind?”, “Leave without saving?”. The body says exactly what is lost and what is not. The buttons repeat the verbs: “Stay” / “Leave Northwind”, “Discard and leave” / “Keep editing”. Never “Are you sure?”. The invite dialog says what it is for and for which team, “Invite teammates to Northwind”, and repeats that only a teammate’s city is ever shown.
