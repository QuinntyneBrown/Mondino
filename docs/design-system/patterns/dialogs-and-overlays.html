Pattern

Dialogs and overlays

Mondino interrupts twice only. Everything else happens in place.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

A dialog stops everything, so it is kept for the two moments when stopping is kind: before a pin is removed and a team left, and before unsaved settings are dropped.

When to use

NeedUseNot
Confirm removing your pinA dialog with a danger buttonA second click on the same link
Leaving with unsaved changesA dialog; the safe choice is primarySilently dropping them
Show a teammateThe panel, in place beside or under the globeA dialog or a popover over the globe
Confirm a copyA toastA dialog
Say a setting is unsavedThe save barA dialog
Edit somethingA screen (Profile & Settings)An inline form or a hand-rolled modal

Structure

Leave without saving?

Your changes to temperature and clock haven’t been saved. If you leave now, they’re dropped.

A question, what is lost, the safe choice and the other one

States

  • Open: focus on the safe action; the page behind is inert and dimmed.
  • Busy: the confirming button is busy; Cancel is disabled; nothing can be sent twice.
  • Failed: an alert at the top of the dialog; nothing has changed; “Try again”.
  • Done: leaving goes to a page that confirms it. Only one dialog is ever open.

Responsive behaviour

Under 576 px a dialog is a sheet from the bottom edge with stacked, full-width buttons, the confirming action on top. From 576 px it is centred with its buttons in a row.

Accessibility

  • role="dialog", aria-modal="true", named by its title and described by its body.
  • Focus is kept inside; Escape and Cancel close it and return focus to what opened it; nothing has changed.
  • Built on CDK Dialog. Never hand-roll a modal.
  • Layers come from tokens: backdrop, dialog, toast.

Content

The title is the question and names the thing. The body says exactly what is lost and what can be undone. The buttons repeat the verbs. Never “Are you sure?”.
