Pattern

Empty and error states

What Mondino says when there is nothing to show, when it could not load, and when a screen is not for this person.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

An empty screen still tells the truth and offers a way forward. Mondino distinguishes four cases and words each differently.

When to use

CaseWhat it saysWhere
A team of one“Just you, for now”, with the invite link to copyGlobe, Team
A filter found nothingOne line in the list’s own words; the filter stays in viewTeam, Moments
Nothing exists yetWhat will appear and whenMoments with nothing coming up
Could not load“We couldn’t load…”, that nothing is lost, “Try again”Every screen
Not for youWhat you need: an invite linkAny address, for someone in no team
GoneWhat happened and how to come backAfter leaving; a link that cannot be used; an address that leads nowhere

Structure

We couldn’t load your team

Mondino didn’t answer just now. Nothing is lost — your pin and your team are as you left them.

States

The header stays in every case where the person is a teammate, so the other screens are one step away. A page for someone in no team has the bare header and shows no team’s name, count or pins.

Responsive behaviour

Centred under the header, at most --layout-container-message wide, at every width. Buttons wrap.

Accessibility

  • When the message is the whole page, its title is the <h1>.
  • A filter’s empty line is a polite status, so choosing the filter is answered aloud.
  • An error page’s “Try again” is a button; “Back to the globe” is a link.

Content

Never a status code, never “Something went wrong” alone, never blame. An invite link that cannot be used says it may have been withdrawn, and whom to ask. A lookup that failed says the postal code may be fine.
