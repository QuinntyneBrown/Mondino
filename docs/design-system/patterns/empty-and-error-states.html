Pattern

Empty and error states

What Mondino says when there is nothing to show, when a filter or a search found nothing, when it could not load, and when a screen is not for this person.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

An empty screen still tells the truth and offers a way forward. Mondino distinguishes six cases and words each differently. A teammate inside a team keeps the header; someone outside one gets a page that says one thing.

When to use

States

The header stays in every case where the person is a teammate, so the other screens are one step away. A page for someone on no team has no header, only the wordmark, and shows no team’s name, count or pins; the one exception is “Northwind is full”, which names the team because the invite link was good. A screen that could not load keeps nothing half-drawn: the empty state takes its whole place.

Responsive behaviour

An empty state is centred under the header, at most --layout-container-message wide, at every width; a notice page is centred in the screen under its wordmark. Buttons wrap. The card for a team of one stacks its button under its text under 576 px.

Accessibility

  • When the message is the whole page, its title is the <h1>.
  • A filter’s or a search’s empty result is a polite status, so choosing the filter or typing is answered aloud.
  • An error page’s “Try again” is a button; “Back to the globe” is a link.
  • When a notice takes a form’s place, focus moves to its title.

Content

Never a status code, never “Something went wrong” alone, never blame. An error’s title names what did not load: “The globe didn’t load”, “The team didn’t load”, “Moments didn’t load”, “Your settings didn’t load”; its text says what is unharmed: “Check your connection — nothing you set has been lost.” An invite link that cannot be used says why and whom to ask: “Someone on the team reset it, so it can’t be used to join. Ask whoever sent it for the new link.” A lookup that failed says the postal code may be fine.

A filter that found nothing answers in its own words: “No one is asleep right now”; with no one working, “No one is in working hours right now” / “It’s the quiet gap between time zones. Check back in an hour.”; “No sport moments coming up”; “No one matches “Reykjavik””.
