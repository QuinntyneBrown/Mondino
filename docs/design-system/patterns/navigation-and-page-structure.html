Navigation and page structure

A handful of screens and one header. The whole app stays this small.

Overview

Before a team there are three screens with no header: Join, Sign In and Create Team. Inside a team there are four, Globe, Team, Moments and Profile & Settings, all reachable from every screen through the app header, which also carries “Invite teammate”. Three pages say one thing each: Left Team, Invite Needed and Not Found. There is no sidebar, no breadcrumb and no menu, because there is nothing deeper to reach.

When to use

ToUse
Move between screensThe header’s tabs and the avatar
Invite a teammate“Invite teammate” in the header, on any screen: it opens the invite dialog and leaves the screen where it is
Open a teammateA pin, a panel row, a Team row, a ribbon marker or a moment: each opens Globe with that pin selected
Go back from a teammate or a groupThe panel’s close button, or Escape
Narrow a listA segmented control (Team), chips (Moments), or the search above a large team, on the same screen
Get in from outsideThe footer under each form: “Sign in”, “Start a team”; the actions of a notice page

Structure

The header
  • Each screen has one <h1> and, under it, a line with the numbers that matter.
  • Every screen has its own address. Opening or reloading it shows that screen, and the back button returns to the last one.
  • A selected teammate is in the address as an opaque id: no name, city or postal code.
  • Every route loads only when it is opened; the globe’s renderer loads only when a globe is first drawn.

States

  • Current screen: its tab has aria-current="page" and an ink fill; on Profile & Settings the avatar is ringed in pin.
  • Before a team: no header. Join, Sign In and Create Team show the wordmark over their form.
  • On no team: any screen’s address shows “You need an invite link”, in place.
  • Just left: “You’ve left Northwind”, with the ways to start or sign in to another team.
  • An address that leads nowhere: “This page fell off the map”, with the address as text and “Back to the globe”.
  • Already a teammate: opening the team’s invite link goes to Globe; no second pin is made.

Responsive behaviour

The header keeps its tabs at every width, in reach of a thumb, each with a 44 px target. Under 576 px the wordmark goes and the mark stays; under 768 px “Invite teammate” is its icon alone. See page templates for each screen’s layout.

Accessibility

  • Landmarks: one header, one primary navigation, one main. The skip link comes first.
  • Moving to a new screen moves focus to its heading and announces its title.
  • The page’s title names the screen and the product.

Content

The tabs are “Globe”, “Team”, “Moments”. The button is “Invite teammate”. The avatar’s link is named “Profile & Settings”. Headings say what the screen answers, not what it is called: “Where everyone is in their day”.
