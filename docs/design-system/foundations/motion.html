Foundation

Motion

Little moves, and for a reason: the globe turns, a pin lifts, a panel fades, a new pin pulses once.

Status: stableSince v1.0
  • Overview
  • Durations and easings
  • The globe
  • Usage
  • Reduced motion
  • Tokens

Overview

Mondino is left open all day in a tab beside the work, so it is calm. Motion shows where something came from or that something changed, and then stops.

Durations and easings

TokenUsed for
--duration-fast 150 msHover and focus colour; a pin lifting; a switch’s thumb
--duration-base 180 msThe panel fading from one teammate to the next; a toast and the save bar arriving
--duration-slow 320 msA dialog or a sheet
--duration-deliberate 1600 msA new pin’s one pulse; a skeleton’s pulse; one turn of a spinner
--ease-standardEverything by default: quick out, gentle in

The globe

  • Left alone, the globe turns slowly eastward. Dragging spins it, and it stays put for 5 seconds after.
  • Selecting a pin turns the globe to face it within 1 second, and it holds there until the selection is cleared.
  • A pin lifts within 200 ms of being pointed at or focused, and settles back when the pointer leaves.
  • A newly placed pin pulses once.
  • While its tab is hidden the globe neither turns nor draws.

Usage

  • Use a duration token; never write a time in a component.
  • Nothing flashes more than three times in a second.
  • Nothing animates when a page loads except a skeleton’s pulse.
  • A local time ticking over is a change of text, not an animation.

Reduced motion

When the system asks for reduced motion, tokens.css sets every duration to nothing, so components need no rule of their own. Beyond that: the globe does not turn on its own and shows a selected pin facing the viewer at once; the panel changes with no fade; a new pin does not pulse; a spinner stands still. The app follows the setting as it changes, with no reload.
