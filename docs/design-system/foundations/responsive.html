Foundation

Responsive

Every screen works from the narrowest phone to the widest desktop. No screen is built for one size and patched for the rest.

Status: stableSince v1.0
  • Overview

Overview

Base styles are for a phone. Each breakpoint adds room: a wordmark, columns, the panel beside the globe. The breakpoints and page templates are on Layout; this page says what each component does as the width changes.

Touch and pointer

  • Every touch target is at least 44 × 44 px, whatever size the control is drawn. A pin’s hit area is 44 px even when the pin is 18 px.
  • Nothing depends on hover. A pin lifts on tap as it does on hover; a tap elsewhere on the globe clears it.
  • The globe spins with one finger. A vertical drag it cannot follow scrolls the page, so the globe never traps a phone.
  • Turning a phone keeps the selected pin, any filter and anything typed.

Testing

Look at every screen in every state at 320, 375, 576, 768, 992, 1200 and 1920 px, in both themes. Check: no sideways scroll, nothing clipped or overlapping, targets at least 44 px, a dialog becomes a sheet, the panel moves under the globe.
