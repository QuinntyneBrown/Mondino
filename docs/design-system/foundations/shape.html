Foundation

Shape

Soft and round, like the globe: pills for anything pressed, generous corners on surfaces.

Status: stableSince v1.0
  • Overview
  • Radii
  • Borders
  • Usage
  • Tokens

Overview

The radius grows with the thing. Controls a teammate presses are full pills. Inputs and rows are 12 px. Cards are 16 px. The largest surfaces, the pin card, today’s moment and a sheet, are 24 px.

--radius-sm 4 pxCode, a legend swatch, the bars inside the overlap track
--radius-md 8 pxSkeleton lines, the overlap track, the invite card’s Copy button
--radius-lg 12 pxInputs, rows, moment cards, alerts, the date block
--radius-xl 16 pxCards, the day ribbon, a toast
--radius-2xl 24 pxThe pin card, today’s moment, a dialog, the save bar
--radius-fullButtons, chips, segmented controls, pins, avatars, status, pills

Borders

A hairline separates; it is decorative and does not need 3:1. The edge of a control does, and uses --color-border-strong. A selected pin’s ring is 3 px so it reads on the globe.

Usage

  • Anything a teammate presses that is not a row is a pill.
  • Do not mix radii on one surface: a card’s inner well is one step smaller than the card.
  • The extraction dropped a 2 px radius the mocks used four times; it is folded into --radius-sm.
