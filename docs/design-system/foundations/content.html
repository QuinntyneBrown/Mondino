Content

How Mondino speaks: plainly, warmly, in the words the team already uses.

Overview

Mondino answers one question: what is it like where my teammate is, right now? Its words are short and concrete. It knows clocks, weather and calendars; it does not know what anyone is doing, and never pretends to.

Style

  • Sentence case for everything: headings, buttons, labels, tabs.
  • Buttons are verbs that name the thing: “Place my pin”, “Join Northwind”, “Save changes”. Never “OK”, “Submit”, “Yes”.
  • Labels are nouns: “Your name”, “Country”, “Postal code”.
  • Speak to the teammate as “you”, and of Mondino as “we” only when something went wrong on our side: “We couldn’t load your team.”
  • Typographic apostrophes and quotation marks; an ellipsis character for something in progress.

Formulas

An error

What happened, what it means for you, what to do. If a source failed, say it could not be checked, not that you were wrong.

We couldn’t find “2012” in Italy. Italian postal codes have five digits, like 20121.

Names the postal code, the country and the form to use

Nothing to show

What will appear here, and when. For a filter, one line in the list’s own words.

Everyone is awake right now.

Something destructive

A question that names the thing; exactly what is lost and what is not; buttons that repeat the verb.

What a teammate writes

A teammate’s name, a team’s name, and a city or a moment’s title from an outside source are data. They are shown as text, never as HTML, wherever they appear. A moment’s title is cut at 80 characters and its detail at 160, with an ellipsis.
