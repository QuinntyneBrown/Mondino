Foundation

Elevation

Mondino is mostly flat. A shadow means something is in front: a lifted pin, a card over the globe, the save bar, a dialog.

Status: stableSince v1.0
  • Overview
  • Levels
  • Surfaces
  • Z-order
  • Usage
  • Tokens

Overview

Surfaces are told apart by a hairline and a step in colour, not by shadow. Shadows are kept for the few things that really sit above the page.

LevelUsed by
--shadow-1A pin label; the switch’s thumb
--shadow-2A mock note; kept for a small raised surface
--shadow-3The invite card over the globe; today’s moment on hover
--shadow-4A dialog, a toast, the save bar
--shadow-liftA pin that is pointed at, focused or selected, and its lifted label
--shadow-focusA focus ring drawn as a shadow, where an outline cannot be used

In dark, surfaces get lighter as they rise: canvas, then surface, then raised. Shadows are darker and stronger, and do less of the work.

LayerTokenHolds

Usage

  • A component never writes a raw z-index; it reads a layer.
  • Do not add a shadow to make something look important. Use weight, size or position.
  • The globe’s own drop shadow is part of its picture, not an elevation level.
