Foundation

Typography

Gabarito for the wordmark, names and clocks. Inter for everything that is read. Times and temperatures use tabular figures.

Status: stableSince v1.0
  • Overview
  • Families
  • Roles
  • Figures

Overview

Two families, both bundled with the app and never loaded from a CDN. Gabarito is the display face: the wordmark, headings, a teammate’s name, a local time, a temperature. Inter is for labels, body text and captions. Body text is 16 px and nothing a teammate must read is under 12 px.

Figures

Every time and temperature carries .tnum (font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums), so a clock does not shift as it ticks from 11:11 to 12:00.

Usage

  • A page’s title is 24 px on a phone, 30 px from 576 px and 36 px from 992 px. Join’s headline is 36 px, then 48 px from 576 px.
  • Use a role (--text-h2), never a size and a weight written by hand.
  • The two smallest sizes, 11 px and 10 px, are for a pin’s initials, a pin label and the marks under a bar or the ribbon. They never carry a sentence.
  • Headings balance their lines; paragraphs keep to --layout-measure.
  • Sentence case everywhere. The wordmark is lowercase.

Accessibility

Sizes are in rem, so they follow the reader’s own text size. At 200% text size every screen keeps its content and controls with no sideways scrolling at 1280 px. Line height is 1.5 for text that is read. Text is never an image.
