Foundation

Colour

Paper and ink carry the interface. Pin marks people, sun marks moments, leaf marks working hours. The globe has colours of its own. Nothing else competes.

Status: stableSince v1.0
  • Overview
  • Primitives
  • Roles

Overview

Mondino’s palette is small on purpose. A lavender paper canvas, white surfaces and hairlines, and deep indigo ink for text, navigation and the one main action on a screen. Three colours carry meaning and are used for nothing else: pin for people, sun for moments, leaf for working hours. Pin is violet, sun yellow and leaf teal. A fourth, sea, is the brand’s deep indigo.

Colours live in three tiers: primitives (--palette-*) hold the values, semantic tokens (--color-*) give them a role, and components read only the roles.

Light and dark are both designed. The dark theme is the prototype’s own: its canvas, surfaces, hairlines and ink, a pin, a sun and a leaf each a step brighter, and a deeper globe.

Usage

  • Pin is for people: a selected pin’s ring, the overlap bar, your avatar’s ring on Profile & Settings. As text it is --color-fg-people, a darker pin.
  • Sun is for moments: a moment marker, an emblem, a moment card, today’s moment. It also tints the early-morning and evening status, the edges of the working day.
  • Leaf is for working hours: the status, the day ribbon’s band, a switch that is on.
  • Ink is for the one main action, the chosen tab or filter, and text. A danger action uses the danger role, which is in pin’s family.
  • Colour never carries a meaning alone: a status has its words, a chosen option its tick or fill and an attribute, an error its icon and text.
  • Components read --color-* only. A primitive in a component is a bug.
