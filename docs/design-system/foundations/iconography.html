Foundation

Iconography

Line icons on a 24-unit grid that take the colour of the text beside them: the weather, the four kinds of moment, and a few signs.

Status: stableSince v1.0
  • Overview
  • Weather
  • Moments
  • Interface
  • Sizes
  • Usage

Overview

Icons are from Lucide (ISC licence), drawn inline as SVG with a 1.75 px stroke, round caps and joins, and currentColor. They are bundled with the app. An icon supports words; it rarely replaces them.

Weather

Eight conditions, each an icon with its word. A clear or partly cloudy sky uses its night form when the teammate is probably asleep.

ClearClear, at nightPartly cloudyPartly cloudy, at nightCloudyRainyShowersStormyFoggyWindy

Moments

One icon per kind, always the same, shown on sun as an emblem. A team’s or a series’ own logo is used only where Mondino holds the right to it.

Sizes

36 px beside a temperature in the panel; 20 px by default; 16 px in a button, a message or a row; 14 px in a pin label, a chip or a pill

Usage

  • An icon beside its word is decorative: aria-hidden="true".
  • An icon that stands alone carries its meaning as role="img" with an aria-label (a weather icon in the Team table), or its button carries the label (close, dismiss).
  • A weather icon is always available as a word too: in the panel beside it, or in the pin’s name.
  • Do not recolour an icon to carry a meaning; it takes the colour of its text.
